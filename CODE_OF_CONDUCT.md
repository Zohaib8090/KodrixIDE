# Code of Conduct

Kodrix is built in the open by people with very different backgrounds, ages, languages and
levels of experience. Plenty of people here are writing their first issue or their first
pull request. We want every one of them to feel welcome and safe.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and the project owner, pledge to make taking part in Kodrix a
harassment-free experience for everyone. That applies regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, social or economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

**Behavior that helps:**
- Being kind and patient, especially with beginners and people writing in a second language
- Respecting different opinions, experiences and ways of working
- Giving feedback about the code, not the person, and explaining the reasons behind it
- Accepting feedback gracefully, and admitting and learning from mistakes
- Crediting other people's work and ideas
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it

**Behavior that is not acceptable:**
- Insults, name-calling, or personal or political attacks
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind
- Harassment in public or private, including repeatedly contacting someone who asked you
  to stop
- Publishing someone's private information (address, phone number, email, real name)
  without their explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spamming issues and pull requests
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in every Kodrix space: this repository's issues, pull
requests, discussions and wiki, the `KodrixMarketplace` repository, and any other official
Kodrix channel. It also applies when someone represents the project in public, for example
when posting as Kodrix on social media.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project owner,
[@Zohaib8090](https://github.com/Zohaib8090), by email at
**zohaibbaig144@gmail.com**. Put "Kodrix Code of Conduct" in the subject line.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The owner will respect the privacy
and safety of the person who reported the incident.

Please don't use the security vulnerability form for conduct reports. That form is only
for security problems in the software.

## Enforcement

The project owner decides what counts as a violation. Depending on how serious it is, one of
these steps will be taken:

1. **Correction.** For a minor slip, such as unprofessional language: a private message
   explaining what went wrong. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single more serious incident, or repeated minor ones: a warning with
   consequences for continuing. There's no interaction with the people involved for a set
   time.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, or continued behavior after a warning: a
   temporary ban from all interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of a person, or aggression
   toward or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from the project.

The project owner may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this Code of Conduct.
